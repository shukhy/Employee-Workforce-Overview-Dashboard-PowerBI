# 👥 Employee Workforce Overview Dashboard | Power BI

## 📖 Project Overview

Organizations generate large volumes of workforce data, but transforming that information into meaningful business insights is essential for effective Human Resource management. This project was developed to analyze employee demographics, workforce trends, compensation, job satisfaction, employee retention, and organizational performance through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The Employee Workforce Overview Dashboard provides HR professionals and business leaders with a centralized view of workforce metrics, helping them identify trends, monitor employee engagement, and support data-driven workforce planning.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Human Resource teams often face challenges in answering key workforce questions:

- How has employee hiring changed over time?
- What is the current workforce composition?
- What is the employee termination rate?
- How are salaries distributed across different employee groups?
- What factors influence employee satisfaction and retention?
- How does education level impact workforce distribution?
- Are there trends that require management attention?

Without a centralized reporting platform, discovering these insights can be time-consuming and inefficient.

---

## 🚀 Solution

Developed a comprehensive Power BI dashboard to provide:

✅ Workforce Overview

✅ Employee Demographic Analysis

✅ Recruitment & Hiring Trends

✅ Employee Satisfaction Monitoring

✅ Salary & Compensation Analysis

✅ Termination & Retention Insights

✅ Work-Life Balance Assessment

✅ Workforce Performance Reporting

# 🔍 Key Insights

- Workforce consists of **1,233 employees**
- Total payroll expenditure exceeds **$8 million**
- Employee satisfaction is strongest within the High and Very High categories
- Bachelor's and Master's degree holders represent a significant portion of the workforce
- Employee hiring increased significantly after 2010
- Work-Life Balance is predominantly rated as Better and Good
- Termination trends highlight periods requiring further retention analysis
- Full-time employees account for the majority of salary expenditure
